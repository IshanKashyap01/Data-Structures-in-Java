# Introduction to Recursion

- Recursion is based on **Principal of Mathematical Induction** (PMI)

- PMI is used to prove that a mathematical statement is true for all natural
numbers

## How PMI Works

Suppose you have to prove $f(n) \text{ is true } \forall\ \N$

1. Prove that $f(0)$ and $f(1)$ are true

2. Assuming $f(k)$ is true, prove $f(k + 1)$ is also true
